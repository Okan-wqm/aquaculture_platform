- **Teslimatlar:** Protected categories/version, immutable diff inputs, confidence-independent rules ve prohibited fallback.
- **Test/negative control:** Unknown/mixed change, policy self-edit, path disguise, recursive impact unknown ve stale taxonomy.
- **Evidence class:** `code_proven`, `operator_attested`.
- **Acceptance IDs:** `ACC-S49`, `ACC-TCB-001`.
- **Dependencies:** S48.
- **Finding IDs:** `ARIA-AUDIT-054`, `056`, `068`.
- **Commit/note:** Classifier mutation tests green/pushed; note policy digest ve operator approval taşır.
- **Exit predicate:** Yalnız açık allowlisted semantic class low-risk olur; unknown/mixed/prohibited reddedilir.

## S50 — Single-use merge permit

- **Test/negative control:** Forge/replay/double consume, base/head/payload swap, expiry/revoke ve cross-workspace.
- **Evidence class:** `code_proven`, `operator_attested`.
- **Acceptance IDs:** `ACC-S50`, `ACC-SEP-001`, `ACC-TCB-001`.
- **Dependencies:** S49.
- **Finding IDs:** `ARIA-AUDIT-012`–`015`.

- **Exit predicate:** Permit yalnız bir exact durable merge effect yaratır; yeniden kullanım imkânsızdır.
