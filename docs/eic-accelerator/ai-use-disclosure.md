# Disclosure of AI tool use (required by the Part B template, p.15 to p.16)

The template requires applicants to verify AI generated content and citations, to list the sources,
to check for reproduced text, to acknowledge the limits of the tool, and to disclose which AI tools
were used and how. This note is the draft of that disclosure. The company reviews and signs off the
final text.

## Draft text for the proposal

> The technology described here (ARIA) was itself developed with the help of a generative AI coding
> assistant (Anthropic Claude, used through Claude Code) under the direction and review of the
> team. The team also used the same assistant to prepare the technical parts of this proposal from
> the project's repository, its test results and its measurement records. Every factual statement
> in those parts carries a pointer to a repository file, a pull request number or a measurement
> record, and was checked by a person against that source. The assistant can make errors and can
> state unsupported claims with confidence; for that reason each claim is labelled with its
> strength, and claims that are not yet demonstrated are written as plans with dates and
> measurable criteria. The team is responsible for the content.

## What the company must confirm before submission

1. Which people reviewed which statements, and that the review was real (the company signs the
   declaration that the information is correct and complete).
2. The list of sources and citations used in the final text, checked one by one.
3. That market figures, competitor statements and any citation taken from an AI tool were checked
   against the original source.
4. Whether other AI tools were used for the video script, the pitch deck, translations or images.
