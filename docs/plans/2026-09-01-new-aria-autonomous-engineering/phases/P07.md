  ve prohibited fallback.
- **Test/negative control:** Unknown/mixed change, policy self-edit, path disguise, recursive impact
  unknown ve stale taxonomy.
- **Evidence class:** `code_proven`, `operator_attested`.
- **Acceptance IDs:** `ACC-S49`, `ACC-TCB-001`.
- **Dependencies:** S48.
- **Finding IDs:** `ARIA-AUDIT-054`, `056`, `068`.
- **Commit/note:** Classifier mutation tests green/pushed; note policy digest ve operator approval
  taşır.
- **Exit predicate:** Yalnız açık allowlisted semantic class low-risk olur; unknown/mixed/prohibited
  reddedilir.

- **Evidence class:** `code_proven`, `operator_attested`.
- **Acceptance IDs:** `ACC-S50`, `ACC-SEP-001`, `ACC-TCB-001`.
- **Dependencies:** S49, `OP-03`.
- **Finding IDs:** `ARIA-AUDIT-012`–`015`.

  effect taşır.
- **Exit predicate:** Permit producer/consumer tarafından issue edilemez; external issuer yalnız
  admitted exact low-risk effect için tek durable permit üretir ve yeniden kullanım imkânsızdır.

- **Evidence class:** `code_proven`, `live_proven`.
