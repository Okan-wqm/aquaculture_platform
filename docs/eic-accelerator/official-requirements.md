# What the official documents require (digest with page references)

Read on 2026-10-26 from three official documents. Page numbers are the PDF pages.

- **Guide**: EIC Accelerator, Guide for applicants, version 6.0, 19 November 2025.
- **WP**: EIC Work Programme 2026 (209 pages).
- **Template**: Short proposal Part B template, version 2.1, 12 June 2026, and the Part A form.

If the portal shows a newer version of any of them, the portal wins.

## 1. Process and dates

| Item                | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                       | Source                    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Short proposal      | May be submitted at any time. It is batched for evaluation on the first Tuesday of every month at 17:00 Brussels time. Feedback comes about 4 to 6 weeks after the batching date.          | Guide p.35, p.36; WP p.78 |
| Next batching dates | First Tuesdays: 3 November 2026, 1 December 2026, 5 January 2027 (computed from the rule).                                                                                                 | WP p.78                   |
| Registration        | Register on the Funding and Tenders Portal and obtain a 9-digit PIC before starting. Natural persons also need a PIC.                                                                      | Guide p.36                |
| Short evaluation    | Four expert evaluators, each gives GO or NO GO. At least three GO: invited to a full proposal. At least two NO GO: rejected. Feedback from all four.                                       | Guide p.35; WP p.83       |
| After a GO          | Full proposal at a 2026 batch or at a 2027 batch (dates set in the 2027 Work Programme). Three days of free coaching, once.                                                                | WP p.78, p.79; Guide p.40 |
| Full evaluation     | Technology expert plus an evaluation panel of at least three. Online meeting with the technology expert about 3 to 4 weeks after batching. Panel result about 8 to 9 weeks after batching. | Guide p.46, p.48          |
| Jury                | After every second batch, in Brussels, at most 3 representatives, no consultants. 45 minutes: 10 minutes presentation and 35 minutes questions.                                            | Guide p.47, p.51, p.53    |

## 2. Who may apply and which funding

| Item                    | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Source                          |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Eligible applicant      | A single start-up or SME established in an EU Member State or a Horizon Europe Associated Country. Natural persons may apply if the company is established before the contract.                                                                                  | Guide p.9, p.10                 |
| Norway                  | Listed as a Horizon Europe Associated Country. If the association agreement excludes the EIC Accelerator investment component, applicants from that country may only apply for grant only. **To verify for Norway with the national contact point.**             | WP p.176                        |
| One at a time           | No concurrent submission to the Accelerator or the STEP Scale Up call before the earlier result is out.                                                                                                                                                          | Guide p.11; WP p.74             |
| Grant only              | A lump sum of less than EUR 2.5 million (at most EUR 2 499 999), once per legal entity for the whole of Horizon Europe (2021 to 2027). Covers at most 70% of eligible costs of TRL 6 to 8 activities. The beneficiary pays the other 30% from its own resources. | Guide p.13, p.15; WP p.75, p.76 |
| Grant only, extra proof | Evidence of sufficient financial means to deploy and scale (for example revenue or existing investors). The evaluators ask whether the applicant can show access to the resources to commercialise and scale up, and whether the grant is needed.                | Guide p.15; WP p.87             |
| Blended finance         | The grant above plus an investment of EUR 1 to 10 million, usually equity or quasi-equity, at most 25% of the voting shares, patient capital view of 7 to 10 years. A work package cannot be funded by a mix of both.                                            | Guide p.13, p.16                |
| Duration                | Innovation activities normally within 24 months, longer only if well justified.                                                                                                                                                                                  | WP p.77                         |
| Need for EU support     | The company must show it is not investment-ready under the current market risk appetite.                                                                                                                                                                         | Guide p.44                      |

## 3. The short application (what to prepare)

| Part            | Content and limits                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Source                        |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Part A (portal) | Acronym, title (at most 200 characters, understandable to non-specialists), abstract (2000 characters), keywords (up to three from the taxonomy, free keywords up to 200 characters), duration in months, whether a similar proposal was submitted in the past two years, the PIC, CEO data, requested amount, nine declarations. | Template p.4, p.5; Guide p.37 |
| Part B (PDF)    | **At most 12 pages including the cover.** Sections: 1 Technology, 2 Market, 3 Team, financial needs and implementation. The document stands alone: links are ignored, and extra pages are made invisible.                                                                                                                         | Template p.15, p.17 to p.19   |
| Format          | Times New Roman or equivalent, body text at least 11 pt, A4, margins at least 15 mm, single line spacing at least.                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Template p.15                 |
| Pitch deck      | PDF, at most 10 slides, no template. Two to four slides should carry business model, target market and growth forecast.                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Guide p.37; Template p.20     |
| Video           | At most 3 minutes, core team (up to three people). It shows the team and their motivation. Any EU language is allowed.                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Guide p.38                    |
| NCP consent     | Consent for data sharing with the National Contact Point, on the template.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Template p.20                 |

Questions in Part B (Template p.18 to p.19):

1. **Technology.** Novelty and breakthrough nature (deep tech; the improvement in cost or
   performance against alternatives; empirical data that show the novelty). TRL: evidence of TRL 5
   or higher validation. IP: protection and strategy (patents granted or pending, trade secrets,
   other IP elements, freedom to operate status).
2. **Market.** Total market and the realistic share; go-to-market and commercialisation; customer
   value proposition, partnerships, who the customers are and why they will adopt.
3. **Team, financial needs, implementation.** Key team members and competences; governance and
   decision making; critical gaps including gender balance and the plan to fill them; traction
   with investors, timing and size of the next financing round; why EIC funding is needed and why
   market actors will not fund the full amount.

## 4. What the short evaluators score

Only a subset of the criteria is assessed at the short stage (WP p.85 to p.87).

| Criterion                                                                            | Short stage      | Full stage                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Deep tech and breakthrough nature                                                    | Assessed         | Assessed                                 |
| TRL: all aspects of TRL 5 completed (validation in a relevant environment)           | Assessed         | Assessed                                 |
| IP protection and strategy                                                           | Assessed         | Assessed                                 |
| Market opportunity                                                                   | Assessed         | Assessed                                 |
| Business model and revenue strategy, financial projections                           | **Not assessed** | Assessed                                 |
| Broader impact, European leadership                                                  | **Not assessed** | Assessed                                 |
| Team capability (and gender balance, gaps)                                           | Assessed         | Assessed, plus governance and incentives |
| Risk level of the investment and leverage effect (investor traction, financing need) | Assessed         | Assessed                                 |
| Risk management; implementation plan; lump-sum costs                                 | **Not assessed** | Assessed                                 |

Full proposal: 0 to 5 per criterion, minimum 4 per criterion and 13 of 15 overall for an interview.
The "TRL 6 to 8" wording of the funding is to be read as: the applicant has completed all aspects of
TRL 5 (WP p.77, footnote 111).

## 5. Limits and consequences

- **Three unsuccessful submissions of the same or an improved proposal by the same legal entity,
  at any stage including the short proposal, close the Accelerator under Horizon Europe for that
  entity.** Applicants are expected to reapply only after significant improvements
  (WP p.87; Guide p.49). A submission can be withdrawn before the result without counting
  (Guide p.45).
- **Claims are checked.** The technology expert assesses the claims against third party
  information and in a meeting with the applicant, who may show a prototype or a demo
  (WP p.84; Guide p.47).
- **Misrepresentation.** The declarations state that the information is correct and complete, and
  that false statements or incorrect information may lead to administrative sanctions under the EU
  Financial Regulation (Template p.5). An investment may be rejected and a grant agreement
  suspended or terminated in cases of misrepresentation by the applicant (WP p.82).
- **Civil use only** is declared in Part A (Template p.5).

## 6. Use of generative AI in the proposal (Template p.15 to p.16)

Applicants are fully responsible for the content, including parts produced with an AI tool. They
must verify accuracy and citations, give a list of sources, check for reproduced text, acknowledge
the tool's limits, and **disclose which AI tools were used and how**. A disclosure text is in
`ai-use-disclosure.md`.

## 7. The full proposal adds (Guide p.41 to p.42; WP p.79 to p.80)

A form of up to 20 pages, a new pitch deck, the video, financial data and the cap table, the IP
strategy with a freedom to operate analysis if available, an implementation plan with milestones, a
lump sum budget table, letters of intent and links to the CVs of key personnel.
