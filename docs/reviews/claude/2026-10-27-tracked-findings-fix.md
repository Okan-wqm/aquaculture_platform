## PLAT-HIGH-937 — event-bus handlers on RLS-pool services without the tenant handler scope: admin-api-service, auth-service, billing-service

- **Severity:** HIGH; owner: Okan-wqm; deadline: 2026-11-03.
- **Evidence:** `tests/invariants/event-bus-handler-scope.spec.ts` holds the three services as known gaps. Each registers bus handlers; none binds `TenantEventHandlerScope`.
- **Rule:** a global-schema RLS service binds the scope on the bus, or its handlers are shown not to touch tenant-bound tables.
- **Not verified:** no RLS refusal appears in 24 hours of their logs, because the events may not have occurred. Each needs its handlers read.

## PLAT-HIGH-938 — application code still iterates tenant schemas by name under FORCE RLS in seven files

- **Severity:** HIGH; owner: Okan-wqm; deadline: 2026-11-03.
- **Evidence:** `tests/invariants/tenant-schema-iteration-binds-tenant.spec.ts` holds `cron-jobs.service.ts` (8 jobs), hr-service leave accrual and certification expiry, messaging-service retention, partition manager and knowledge extraction, and sensor-service continuous aggregates at their current counts.
- **Rule:** a worker that touches tenant tables binds its tenant; a schema name alone cannot be bound.
- **Care:** `minio.orphan-cleanup` is one of the eight jobs in `cron-jobs.service.ts` and it deletes objects. Converting it activates a destructive job, so it needs its own review. hr, messaging and sensor are not verified on production (the pattern is the same; whether each table is under RLS and which role runs it was not checked).
